Welcome to my studio—where music comes alive! I provide a supportive, creative environment where you can learn alongside others, grow your skills, and express yourself musically.

Music Lessons for all Ages and Abilities

Simply Music Piano Lessons are for people of all ages; Even people with disabilities are more than welcome and will enjoy learning to play the piano using the Simply Music Method.

Unlock your Natural Voice

When you take Voice Lessons at my studio you learn how to enjoy your singing voice. Music Lessons enhance and help develop cognitive skills that will in turn help you though out your life. Come try voice lessons and find your inner beauty; unlock and free your voice!

Hey, I'm Maggie.

From a young age, I have been deeply passionate about music.

Piano: When I started learning traditional methods of piano instruction they did not resonate with me. Later on, I happily discovered Simply Music— a highly effective and enjoyable approach to learning piano that teaches playing the piano from the very first lesson. This method allows students to experience the joy of making music immediately.
Voice Training: I emphasize a natural approach that encourages students to use their innate abilities to create beautiful and authentic music. I cultivated my love for singing while studying with John Huntingrton for four years and Martha Glissmeyer for a little more than 2 years.

I have been professionally teaching voice since 2010 and piano since 2011. It is my sincere privilege to guide students in developing their musical skills and creativity through these proven methods.
